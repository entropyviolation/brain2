# `components/Home/` — Home Dashboard

The Home tab is the default screen each session. The global **BRAIN2** pinned mill title bar lives in `components/AppHeader.tsx` (wired from `app/page.tsx`), not in this folder — Friend / Review / System / Capture. The optional **now** well lives in Capture (idle → hidden; live Working sessions show name, elapsed, Stop, Pause↔Resume). First-wave Names stamps (`data-ui-name` + `data-ui-docs`) sit on `HomeOverview`, `NeedsAttention`, the Home tabs bay, and the Tracking window here; Habits / Plan / To Do / Goals / Tracking interiors stamp their own desks. Click the **photograph** for that friend’s details. The **chat button** asks for a Stardew-style bubble (habit / today's To Do / Next Action / affection / whim). Click the **bubble** for the mission sheet (the task opens item detail on top; Accept until the end of the day; Decline asks for smaller tasks, then a first step, then a reason). Esc / × / outside dismisses the bubble without declining. Gallery **Details** is the same page. Pack friends start unnamed so you can name them. Worn friend persists until Monday. Returning friends may say hi again. Gallery delete is confirmed and stays gone. Plan + future: [`docs/FRIEND_COMPANION.md`](../../docs/FRIEND_COMPANION.md). Text fields (Gallery names included) use navy focus, never WebKit orange. Home combines a header strip (date + hidable overview squares), a **Needs Attention** queue card, and five sub-tabs: **Habits**, **Plan**, **To Do**, **Goals**, and **Tracking**. The overview strip is the same on every sub-tab — not a Habits-only instrument.

**Habits is the favorite Home surface** — most developed, closest-to-perfect. Plan / To Do / Goals / Tracking interiors look at its Habits Tab Control Panel, analog furniture, Willpower gems (**chrome + black-mirror** oval), CRT phosphor, and designrefs skeuomorph ([`docs/DESIGN_STYLE.md` — Look at Habits](../../docs/DESIGN_STYLE.md#look-at-habits--how-to-extend)). Do not clone `.hab95` onto those rooms; steal the language. Repeat milled chrome around a black-mirror well for other precious objects.

**Scale** on the Moon chart is a session powers-of-ten tour (`lib/sky-scale.ts`). One stop for each decade of metres, from a proton near 10^-16 m through a house mouse, a person, and a western gull at Ocean Beach, San Diego (32.75°N, 117.25°W), out to the observable universe near 10^26 m. Each stop shows the power, one object with a real size, and a one-sentence caption. The full-frame card is `home-sky-scale.tsx`. Moon (10^6 m) and Earth (10^7 m) use the photographs in `public/planets`, as projected globes. A person, the western gull, and San Diego are original drawings, because the repo has no photograph of them. The previous object stays at its true size: the gull a few metres from the person, the Moon’s disk on the Earth’s. One element scales from 1 to 0.1 across the decade, so a photograph pulls back into the next photograph. The other full-frame decades are schematics. At 10^26 m the card is a dark field, a circle labeled schematic, and `GalaxySchematic` shrunk to one luminous speck. The default pace is about four seconds a decade. Pause, Play, Slower, Faster, Previous, and Next are the Transport band and are absent when Scale is off. Opening Scale does not pause the chart clock. Decades the glass can hold ease with `approachZoom`. The 10^21 m step still mounts `GalaxySchematic` on the chart, with the solar system as a dot. The tour is not saved, and it does not change the time rate or the view width. The idea is inspiration only: Powers of Ten (Charles and Ray Eames, 1977), the Florida State University optical microscopy primer, and Scale of the Universe.

On the wide star field the Milky Way is a faint band along the galactic plane, behind the Hipparcos stars. The plane is inclined about 60.2° to the ecliptic, and its ascending node is ecliptic longitude 270°, so the band follows those directions and the center lies toward Sagittarius. The Galaxy stop, and the powers-of-ten tour’s 10^21 m Milky Way step, show `GalaxySchematic`: a soft bulge, a dust lane, and spiral arms as a luminous wash. The Sun is a small labeled dot in the Orion spur, about 27,000 light years from the center of a disk about 100,000 light years across and about 1,000 light years thin. The caption calls it a schematic of our galaxy, not a catalog of other galaxies and not a photograph. `galaxyBandAngle` is the inclination the powers-of-ten tour can read.

The Moon detail’s right readout (`.home-sky-readout`) is three bands. **View** holds Inner, Earth, Moon, System, Stars, and Galaxy in one even grid, and **Scale** is one key in that family. **Transport** is Pause, Play, Slower, Faster, Previous, and Next in two even rows of three; that band is not rendered when Scale is off, so the column does not keep a hole. **Body** holds the date, **Now**, **True sizes** as a compact switch, the selected-body card, Stars near, the planet roster, and the This sky checklist, which stays at the foot of the column. Labels stay the house pixel face (`w95fa` via `--w95-font`) and figures stay Karla (`--font-karla`), with tabular numbers so a degree or an AU does not jitter. Field names stay a dimmer phosphor and the values stay bright. View, Transport, True sizes, and roster keys are near-black lacquer with a phosphor label that remains visible when the key is off; the selected key is the CRT well, and **Now** stays a raised metal key with dark ink. The on-screen motion bar uses that same contrast: Play, Reverse, Pause, and Reset stay readable when off (phosphor on a near-black key) and when held (a light label on the CRT well).

## Shared date

The date plate is the wall clock (`useLiveToday`). `lib/use-current-date.ts` is the **selected day** shared by Habits Day View and Hide Completed, Plan, the To Do day lens, Time Grid, Activity Log, Day Log, and the day summary. Overview squares use that same day unless **Widgets → Follow the clock** is on (`widgetsFollowClock` in `brain2-home-widgets`, persist v9, default off). Then the strip reads the wall clock and ticks once a minute; Habits, Plan, To Do, Goals, and Tracking stay on the selected day. Coarse To Do chevrons (week, month, and season) stay local, the way Habits week nav does. The plate stays the wall clock. Choosing another day survives refresh and leaving Home. A cursor left on today catches up when the calendar moves — at midnight, when the window becomes visible, or on the next launch — including after more than one missed night. A saved day from before that choice was recorded catches up once. Main and Tracking sub-tabs persist in `localStorage`. Habits Daily / Weekly / Monthly + the week/month offset persist too.

**Undo.** Cmd/Ctrl-Z (Cmd/Ctrl-Shift-Z to redo) reverses the last Home or Tracking write: a painted or erased block, a week range fill, a sleep log change, a habit cell, completing a to-do, or a Day Log time entry. The stack lives in `lib/action-history.ts` and is wired from `app/page.tsx`. While Tracking is the active Home tab, `Tracking/tracking-undo.ts` listens in the capture phase so Cmd/Ctrl-Z still pops that stack when the timegrid is focused (the header Now dialog arms the same hook while it is open). A stroke on the plot focuses the grid so a search or notes field does not keep the chord. Typing in a field still uses the browser's undo. The time grid commits on that chord; sleep, habits, points, and tasks from the same snapshot follow immediately after, and derived sync stands down until they have landed so the block cannot flicker back. An undone block is tombstoned, so a later persist reload or an entries write that still carries that id cannot paint it again.

**Habits ⇄ Tracking ⇄ Operations.** Tagged time is the join. Tags live on *time*, not on pens: a pen's tags are the shorthand for "always", and a single block may carry extras of its own — four hours of "San Diego Zoo" in the Location scope tagged *Exercise* without every zoo visit assuming the same. A Goal or Yes-No habit (daily, weekly, monthly, or seasonal) can link tags so any minute carrying them, from any pen in any scope, counts toward its goal automatically — per day, or summed across the week, month, or season. Naming a **New tag** on the Tags row or on Auto-fill from Tracking creates it in the one Tracking catalog. The Tags row can also keep a typed name that is not in that catalog. Editing a name or a color on either row, or in the Tracking tag library, updates that same tag. The count stays one name; the minutes stay ids. **Open item in Lists** from the habit window opens that habit’s standing item; Back returns to the habit window. That window leads with Priority and can store an optional why. Completion can listen to several sources in an explicit trust order. An Operation can carry the same tags: **Working on this now** (operation workspace, Tracking `.trk-now-module` under the view switcher, or header Now dialog) paints those minutes onto the grid, so the habit auto-fill and To Do Done rows come along for free. The bridge is `lib/habit-tracking-sync.ts` plus `lib/operation-work-session.ts`. Home → Tracking also has **Working on right now** in that same module: search a pen color (or Create a new name in a view), start at this second, and the timer paints a block of that color (`lib/pen-color-session.ts`). See `Tracking/README.md` and `Habits/README.md`.

**Scopes attach to each other.** The same hours are *Ian's House* in Location and *Social* in Activity; the block editor's **Also happening** section fills the second one in from the first, with the guest list ticked off inline, as a one-off or as a standing rule on the pen.
