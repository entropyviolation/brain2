**Scale** on the Moon chart is a session powers-of-ten tour (`lib/sky-scale.ts`). One stop for each decade of metres, from a proton near 10^-16 m through a house mouse, a person, and a western gull at Ocean Beach, San Diego (32.75°N, 117.25°W), out to the observable universe near 10^26 m. Each stop shows the power, one object with a real size, and a one-sentence caption. The full-frame card is `home-sky-scale.tsx`. Moon (10^6 m) and Earth (10^7 m) use the photographs in `public/planets`, as projected globes. A person, the western gull, and San Diego are original drawings, because the repo has no photograph of them. The previous object stays at its true size: the gull a few metres from the person, the Moon’s disk on the Earth’s. One element scales from 1 to 0.1 across the decade, so a photograph pulls back into the next photograph. The other full-frame decades are schematics. At 10^26 m the card is a dark field, a circle labeled schematic, and `GalaxySchematic` shrunk to one luminous speck. The default pace is about four seconds a decade. Pause, Play, Slower, Faster, Previous, and Next are the Transport band and are absent when Scale is off. Opening Scale does not pause the chart clock. The tour is not saved, and it does not change the time rate or the view width. The idea is inspiration only: Powers of Ten (Charles and Ray Eames, 1977), the Florida State University optical microscopy primer, and Scale of the Universe.

On the wide star field the Milky Way is a faint band along the galactic plane, behind the Hipparcos stars. The plane is inclined about 60.2° to the ecliptic, and its ascending node is ecliptic longitude 270°, so the band follows those directions and the center lies toward Sagittarius. The Galaxy stop, and the powers-of-ten tour’s 10^21 m Milky Way step, show `GalaxySchematic`: a soft bulge, a dust lane, and spiral arms as a luminous wash. The Sun is a small labeled dot in the Orion spur, about 27,000 light years from the center of a disk about 100,000 light years across and about 1,000 light years thin. The caption calls it a schematic of our galaxy, not a catalog of other galaxies and not a photograph. `galaxyBandAngle` is the inclination the powers-of-ten tour can read.

## Shared date

The date plate is the wall clock (`useLiveToday`). `lib/use-current-date.ts` is the **selected day** shared by Habits Day View and Hide Completed, Plan, the To Do day lens, Time Grid, Activity Log, Day Log, and the day summary. Coarse To Do chevrons (week, month, and season) stay local, the way Habits week nav does. The plate stays the wall clock. Choosing another day survives refresh and leaving Home. A cursor left on today catches up when the calendar moves — at midnight, when the window becomes visible, or on the next launch — including after more than one missed night. A saved day from before that choice was recorded catches up once. Main and Tracking sub-tabs persist in `localStorage`. Habits Daily / Weekly / Monthly + the week/month offset persist too.

**Undo.** Cmd/Ctrl-Z (Cmd/Ctrl-Shift-Z to redo) reverses the last Home or Tracking write: a painted or erased block, a week range fill, a sleep log change, a habit cell, completing a to-do, or a Day Log time entry. The stack lives in `lib/action-history.ts` and is wired from `app/page.tsx`. While Tracking is the active Home tab, `Tracking/tracking-undo.ts` listens in the capture phase so Cmd/Ctrl-Z still pops that stack when the timegrid is focused (the header Now dialog arms the same hook while it is open). A stroke on the plot focuses the grid so a search or notes field does not keep the chord. Typing in a field still uses the browser's undo. The time grid commits on that chord; sleep, habits, points, and tasks from the same snapshot follow immediately after, and derived sync stands down until they have landed so the block cannot flicker back. An undone block is tombstoned, so a later persist reload or an entries write that still carries that id cannot paint it again.

The widget grid above the sub-tabs is the **Visual dashboard** (it was the Home overview strip; the root keeps `data-ui-name="Home overview"` and `#layout`). It reads like a vintage digital car dash: small faces you read at a glance, and the depth in each widget's detail. Plan and reasons: [`docs/WIDGETS_REDESIGN.md`](../../docs/WIDGETS_REDESIGN.md).

**Kinds and installed copies.**

- A **kind** is the app, as the Widget gallery lists it (Points, Countdown, Language study). Its manifest is in `lib/widgets/kinds.ts`: name, install rule, the sizes its face can draw, its gear settings, its gallery page, and an optional first-run guide.
- An **installed copy** is one instance of a kind on the dashboard. It has its own id, title, lamp, size, place, and settings (`WidgetCopy` in `lib/widgets/types.ts`). Every face, detail, and gear reads one copy.
- Each kind says how often it installs: `once` (one copy), `perKey` (one copy per language or place), or `many` (any number). Points and Countdown are `many`. Weather, Solar remainder, and Language study are `perKey`. The rest are `once`.
- Kind ids did not change, so a saved layout keeps its widgets. Countdown is still `daysuntil` and Language study is still `vocab`.
- A copy of a kind this build does not know is kept, never dropped, and draws a quiet `.home-tile.is-unknown` face.
- A user-made kind will register through `registerWidgetKind` under a `user:<slug>` id. **Planned:** nothing user-made ships yet.

**Face, detail, gear.**

- **Face.** Clicking a face opens its detail. Today's tiles are the faces, and their handheld dialogs are the details.
- **Detail.** The silver handheld (`home-widget-dialog.tsx`) is the frame. A kind can use any UI inside; Moon and Language study use a wide sheet.
- **Gear.** Inside a copy, the detail caption has a gear (`.vd-gear`). It swaps the detail for that copy's settings (`widgets/copy-settings.tsx`), with **Done** to return. Settings save to that copy alone, so two Points copies can show different periods.
- The gear lists the kind's own fields first (see the table below), then the face (title, lamp, footer, size), the day it reads (same as the dashboard, the wall clock, or the day being viewed), and the copy itself (show the guide again, hide, remove).
- Every setting changes what the face or detail shows. `widgets/visual-dashboard.test.tsx` checks that.
- **First-run guide.** A kind with a `guide` (Points, Moon, Countdown, Language study) shows a few cards the first time a copy's detail opens (`widgets/widget-guide.tsx`). **Skip** is always there, and `guideSeen` keeps it from coming back.

**Styles.**

- **Milled** is the look the strip had before: the satin handheld bodies, nameplates, and CRTs of `home-widget-face.css`. A vault that already had a saved strip opens on Milled.
- **Dash** is the new car-dash look, on a grid of small cells. A fresh vault opens on Dash.
- Each style keeps its own order and sizes, so switching never loses the other layout.
- Both styles are permanent options. Keep Dash and Milled working in every future version; never remove either, and new kinds should draw in both (a kind with no Dash face falls back to its Milled tile).
- The Dash faces are in `dash/` (`dash-faces.tsx`, registered through `widgets/dash-face-registry.ts`); see [`dash/README.md`](dash/README.md). The Milled / Dash switch is in the Widgets popover.

**Edit mode.**

- Moving and resizing work only in edit mode (`widgets/edit-mode.ts`). Edit mode is not saved, so a reload is always locked.
- In edit mode each cell gets a grip, a corner resize handle that snaps to the kind's sizes, and ×. Clicking a face does not open its detail.
- Keyboard: arrows move the focused copy, and Shift+arrows resize it.
- Drag uses pointer events, with no drag library (`widgets/dashboard-cell.tsx`).
- The **Edit** key on the date plate, beside the Widgets key, turns edit mode on (`home-widgets-menu.tsx`).

**Where it is saved.**

- The dashboard is one blob, `brain2-home-dashboard` (`lib/widgets/dashboard-store.ts`): copies, unknown copies, the style, and the Dash and Milled layouts. See [`lib/README.md`](../../lib/README.md#widgets-libwidgets).
- The first load builds it from the old stores (`dashboardFromLegacy`). Each old id becomes a copy, each Days Until mark a Countdown copy, and each Vocab language a Language study copy. Only the active language shows.
- Every change still writes `order` and `hidden` back to `brain2-home-widgets`, so an older build opens the same strip.
- `home-dashboard` is in the full backup and live restore (`lib/data/backup.ts`, label Visual dashboard). **Reset to my old layout…** in the gallery rebuilds the old strip's showing set and order in Milled, without uninstalling anything.

**Widget gallery.** The Widgets popover opens the **Widget gallery** (`home-widget-catalog.tsx` around `widgets/gallery/`; see [`widgets/gallery/README.md`](widgets/gallery/README.md)). It starts with **How widgets work**, then one page per kind: what it does, how it works, sample faces at each size, its settings, its installed copies, and Install. Countdown, Language study, Weather, and Solar remainder ask a few setup questions first (`hasSetup`). A kind with a guide can play it from its page.

### Countdown

_Last mapped: 2026-10-20_

**Countdown** replaces Days Until and Days Since. Kind id `daysuntil`, installs `many`: one copy per mark.

- Each copy holds `settings.markId`. The mark's fields stay in `brain2-home-days-until` (`lib/home-days-until-store.ts`), and `reconcileCountdowns` keeps marks and copies one to one.
- Kept from Days Until: label, date, optional time, mode (count down to, count up from, or auto), the Plan event link, and the all-day Plan day (`lib/home-days-until-event.ts`).
- **Units:** days, weeks, sleeps, or working days, plus today's unit and decimal faces.
- **Repeats:** yearly or monthly. The face rolls to the next occurrence.
- **Start date:** an optional start draws how far along the span is.
- **Milestones:** a count-up can light its lamp at round numbers (every 100 days, each year).
- **When it passes:** a countdown can stay at 0, switch to counting up, or hide.
- × hides only that copy, not the family. **Remove** in the gear deletes the mark and its copy. The face math is `lib/countdown.ts`. The gear panel is `CountdownSettings` in `home-days-until.tsx`.

### Language study

_Last mapped: 2026-10-20_

**Language study** replaces Vocab. Kind id `vocab`, installs `perKey` by language: one copy per language. The tile is `home-vocab-tile.tsx` with a `lang` prop. The gear panel is `widgets/language-study-settings.tsx`.

- **Role.** A copy's language is `studied` or `native`. English is native by default; the others are studied.
- **Levels.** A studied language sits on the CEFR ladder, A1 to C2 (`lib/widgets/language-levels.ts`). The level picks the word pool: A1 and A2 read common words, B1 everyday words, and B2 and up the harder banks. Setting the level also sets the Vocab bank level (`applyCefrLevel`).
- **Placement quiz.** Optional, from the gear. Each language names the real exam its format follows: DELF / DALF (French), DELE (Spanish), CELI (Italian), Goethe-Zertifikat (German), JLPT (Japanese), HSK (Mandarin), and Cambridge English. The questions are written for Brain2, not copied. The quiz is `language-study/placement-quiz.tsx`, with banks in `lib/language-study/placement/`; it registers through `widgets/placement-registry.ts` and runs from the gear and from install setup.
- **Native languages** get vocab building only: word of the day from Everyday to Extremely advanced, favorites, and flashcards. No CEFR level, no quiz, no 1000 words.
- **Modes.** Vocab is the one mode today: word of the day, 1000 words (studied only), flashcards, and look-up. **Planned:** reading, listening, and grammar modes join as more modes, not more widgets.
- **Face.** Today's word, or the level and a known-word meter (`face`: `word` or `level`).
- **Data stays put.** Favorites and shuffles stay in `brain2-home-vocab`. Known words stay as the `vocab-known` attribute on `vocab-1000-<lang>` items, and My words stay in `vocab-mine-<lang>`.
