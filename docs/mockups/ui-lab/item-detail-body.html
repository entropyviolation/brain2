Plan

Body edits the item’s markdown body. On this task the Studio list shows the panel, so Body leads the bay. The default face is the markdown editor. Source opens a monospace well and the key then reads Rendered. HTML in the body is stripped to plain prose. The editor’s keys are bold, italic, code, heading, lists, quote, and link, then Edit, Split, and Preview. The status line says Markdown, then words and characters. Ctrl or Cmd with B or I wraps the selection. A missing item says Item not found. An empty read-only body says No body yet. An empty preview says Nothing to preview yet.

What stays

The words stay. Source and Rendered stay. The same formatting keys and the three views stay. The count of words and characters stays. The fascia stays: CRT title, Task plate, stage, save lamp, Save, Complete, Missed, Delete, and the other tab labels. Writes still wait about half a second, then flush on blur. Image, embed, and a font menu stay off this tab. They belong to Docs mode.

What changes, and why

The well can take a line length, so a firing log can sit in a column the eye can finish. Heading scale, paper, the quote, and the list ink change the reading and leave the markdown alone. Split can share one gutter, so source and preview are one note. Source can sit with Edit, Split, and Preview, because it is the same kind of choice and today it sits alone above the editor. The status line can quiet down to the word count. The house preset keeps the stacked machine, the white well, and the silver keys.
