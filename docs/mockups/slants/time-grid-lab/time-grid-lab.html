Time Grid studio

Eighteen redesigns of one screen, from keeps every house rule to keeps only the data. Each board is a Hipgnosis idea, a retrofuturist instrument, or both, and each one runs on the same sample week: the Mon Oct 19 and Tue Oct 20 records from the real screen.

Use the bench below. Switching Day or Week, the cell size, the view key or the day redraws every board, so you can compare how each design carries the same minutes. Click a board's keys, pens and tools. Clear day asks first, then offers Undo.

The rule-break dial

Every board states its level. House rules in play are the cool paper and graph rule, silver chrome, no yellow, one color family, doors visible, three type sizes.

0

Keeps every house rule. A Hipgnosis or retro idea in house materials.

1

New furniture the house allows. A new way to read the same minutes.

2

Breaks a few named rules and lists them. Warm light, serif, a face, a diagonal.

3

Re-skins the whole tab. Layout and every control stay.

4

Breaks everything. Only the data, the actions and the states survive.
