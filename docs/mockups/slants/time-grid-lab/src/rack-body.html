Day Tubes

Any day can become a graduated tube of liquid or sand, and turn back into the real grid with one key. It is decorative and optional. The grid keeps every tool and stays the default.

Use Grid, Liquid and Sand above each day. All grid reverts the week. Layers sort by pen, or by time of day so the sand settles like sediment. This mock opens with Mon as sand and Tue as liquid to show it.

How it reads

The side scale is 0 to 24 h. Fill height is tracked minutes over 1440. Layer height is each pen's minutes, or each block in time order. The dashed red line on today's tube is now.

Staying a grid

Days left as Grid keep the paint tools, flags, cell size and Clear day with its confirm. Only the days you turn over change.

Physics

The contents are a real particle simulation in the tube frame. They pour in, settle, slosh when you tilt or shake the tube, and settle again. Turn on Show the math for the equations.
