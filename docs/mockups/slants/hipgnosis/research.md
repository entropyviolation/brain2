# Hipgnosis reference brief for the to-do tab

Hipgnosis was a London studio, 1968 to 1983. Storm Thorgerson and Aubrey "Po" Powell ran it, and Peter "Sleazy" Christopherson joined in 1974 as an assistant and later became a partner. George Hardie drew most of the graphics and logos, and Richard Manning did retouching. The work was photo-led (Hasselblad medium format). They built real staged scenes, then combined them in the darkroom with multiple exposures, airbrush retouching and cut-and-paste. The core method was a verbal or visual pun turned into a literal, dreamlike photo.

## 2. Recurring visual devices

- **One alien object in a real place**: an obelisk, a pig, beds, a cow. Make it small, centred or just off-centre, with heavy negative space.
- **Low horizon and big sky**: the horizon sits in the lower third. Skies are bleached, overcast, or library skies dropped in.
- **Absent or hidden faces**: figures seen from behind, empty suits, veils, smeared or scratched faces.
- **Recursion and doubling**: the Droste picture-in-picture, repeated children, six alternate covers.
- **Staged physical props shot for real**: a real fire, a real diver, a real inflatable pig. The surrealism is literal, not illustrated.
- **Darkroom craft**: multiple exposure, montage seams, airbrush, hand-tinting, Polaroid emulsion smearing, torn-paper overlays. Grain and a slight softness give the period feel. Infrared and solarised looks belong to the wider psychedelic darkroom vocabulary; use them as mood, not as attributed facts.
- **Packaging as a second layer**: black shrink-wrap hiding the art, a round sticker on top, paper bands or obi, gatefolds, inner sleeves that tell more of the story, posters and stickers as inserts, a paper bag, and a sleeve that reacts to water.
- **Black field and one geometric graphic**: Hardie's flat line art (the prism, the handshake, the robot).

## 3. Typography

- Fronts usually had **no type at all** (*Atom Heart Mother*, *Houses*, *Animals*, *DSOTM*, *WYWH*). The title lived on stickers, bands, spines or the back.
- When type appears, it is small, plain, and set at the bottom or a corner: a grotesque sans, sometimes condensed, or Hardie's hand-drawn technical lettering.
- Sticker and label type is circular or banded, set in small caps.
- Google Fonts equivalents:
  - **Inter Tight** or **Archivo** for the plain grotesque.
  - **Archivo Narrow** or **Oswald** (light weights) for condensed labels.
  - **IBM Plex Mono** for captions and credits ("Photography: A. Powell").
  - **Space Grotesk** for a 70s-technical touch.
  - **Caveat** or **Reenie Beanie**, used sparingly, for pencil notes.
- Keep it lowercase or small caps, letter-spacing 0.08 to 0.15em, and 11 to 13px for meta text.

## 5. Rendering in HTML/CSS/SVG (no photos)

1. **Film grain**: an SVG `feTurbulence` (fractalNoise, baseFrequency about 0.8) plus `feColorMatrix` as a fixed overlay, opacity 0.06 to 0.1, `mix-blend-mode: overlay`.
2. **Horizon panels**: `linear-gradient` with a bleached sky down to about 68%, a hard 1px horizon line, then a dark ground. Use one per column header or empty state.
3. **The Object**: a small black CSS or SVG obelisk (a tall rectangle with a soft `drop-shadow`) placed on the horizon. It can mark "today" or the current focus task.
4. **Prism beam**: an SVG triangle outline in `#d9d6cc`, a thin white line in, and six `linear-gradient` bands out. Use it as a progress indicator or a completion animation.
5. **Double exposure**: stack two layers with `mix-blend-mode: screen` or `lighten` and offset opacity. For example, a ghosted task title behind the active one.
6. **Hand-tint and sepia**: on any SVG or illustration, use `filter: sepia(.6) hue-rotate(-15deg) saturate(1.3)`. Add a `radial-gradient` tint wash with `mix-blend-mode: color`.
7. **Polaroid cards**: tasks as cards with a thicker bottom border, `rotate(-1.5deg..2deg)`, and an off-white frame. Smear on drag with `filter: blur(1px)` plus `skewX` (the "Melt" effect).
8. **Sticker and obi overlays**: a round sticker (`border-radius: 50%`, rotated, with an SVG `textPath` for circular type) for counts or tags. A paper band across a column for its title. Wrap a done list in black "shrink-wrap" that slides off on click.
9. **Gatefold**: a two-panel container using `perspective` and `rotateY(-180deg)` with `transform-origin: left` to open the detail view or the inner sleeve.
10. **Recursion**: a Droste-style nested frame for a subtask view (scaled copies with `scale(.6)` inset). Or use scratch/torn-paper reveals with SVG `clip-path` jagged polygons.
11. **Absent face**: avatar slots shown as a silhouette from behind or an empty-suit outline.
