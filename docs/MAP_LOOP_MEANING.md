# Map, loop, meaning

Sep 23, 2026 · how to use three books to perfect Brain2

Brain2 is already a second brain. Perfecting it is not adding rooms. It is
making the house do three things at once, and never confuse them.

A record stays a **map**. The miss between an order and what came back becomes
the **next input**. What lines up without a cause is shown as **meaning**, and
never sold as a reason.

Korzybski, Wiener, and Jung are three instruments on one bench. The product
never names them. The person lives in the house: a sentence that knows its
dates, a day that stays quiet while it holds, a coincidence they may mark.

The work orders stay the work orders. Do not re-sequence them from this brief.

## One house, three jobs

**The map is not the life.** Every Item, painted minute, and habit tick is a
claim about a life, never the life. Claims leave things out. They carry a date.
They sit at an order: what happened, what was written down, what was computed,
what was guessed. Description comes before inference. A plan is drawn beside
what happened and is never merged into it. The next period starts where the
last one stopped.

Without this, the loop steers on a lie, and Analytics writes “you typically”
over a thin week.

**The miss is the next input.** Capture and Tracking are sense organs. The vault
is memory. Plan, Scheduler, To Do, the friend, and BIM act. The house already
senses, stores, and acts. The miss is computed in Analytics and then left
there. Perfecting the loop means one derived **gap** circulates: fast, day, and
slow. Rooms stay wired in parallel on the tag bus. The gap is a second bus, for
errors. It is not an Item. Inside a band, nothing speaks. A day that holds, and
whose blocks have reported, sends no message.

Without this, honesty is a museum.

**A coincidence is shown, never claimed as a cause.** A life is not only a
time series you can average. The same word can appear in a dream, a painted
hour, and an inbox line on one date, with no parent, no dependency, no typed
link. That is a series. The house lists members, dates, observed count, and
the count expected by chance. The person marks **meaningful** or **chance**.
The house never writes that mark. Thin windows stay watermarked. Seasons are
named spans, not a sixth review period. A stuck task may draw a sideways
prompt. Nothing in the card is advice.

Without this, a perfect thermostat still misses a life.

The join is simple. Korzybski says what a record *is*. Wiener says what to *do*
with the difference between intention and that record. Jung says what the
difference must *not* eat.

## How to use them while you build

Read this before you open a slice. Then execute the slice file. If a beautiful
idea here fights a locked decision there, the slice file wins.

### 1. Keep four orders. Never invent a fifth.

**Observed** — an event wrote it (a timestamp, ActivityWatch, a message as
sent, a readback tap).
**Recorded** — the person entered it.
**Derived** — computed (a rollup, a grade, a likely tail, bits of order made).
**Inferred** — guessed (assumed sleep, usual duration).

One mark family. One hover sentence. Compensation, order made, and a likely
overrun are derived. They never overwrite `estimatedDuration`. A typed number
stays recorded. A split reading does not change the winner; it only shows the
minority.

### 2. Index, date, and name what you left out.

Every interpretive sentence carries a subset, a range, and an `n`. Coverage
says the share the claim does not cover. Sample floors stay. A thin window is
not a finding. Copy describes an event (“pushed 9 times since Jul 3”), never
what the person is. Identity labels may remain as ids. They do not remain on
the glass.

### 3. Close the loop at the speed of the thing.

A telltale cannot change a weight. A band cannot move a block. Second-order
changes happen in the weekly review, and only after an accept. Gain runs from
0 to 1. A correction may not push harder than the gap’s gain. After two pushes,
tomorrow is not the default. One effector speaks per gap key per day. Learning
is visible: dated, undoable, never silent.

### 4. Silence is the success test.

Inside a band, the house sends nothing. A coincidence unmarked is not stored.
An exception question is optional. Meaning views can be hidden; capture still
writes. The chrome never lectures. Needles, lamps, and tubes — the language of
Home → Habits. A control diagram does not belong on a screen.

### 5. Leave a window for what will not causalize.

Causal rooms stay causal. Meaning sits beside them as a sixth Analytics group,
on by default. Same room twice is a repeat, not a series. Cross-section
alignment without a shared tag, type, or word stays on Cross-section. No
runtime model judges a coincidence, writes a card, fits a weight, or summarizes
a year. The person is the only one who says a run meant something.

### 6. Isolation is still a bug.

One Item. Modules are not guests. Capture lands through the same writes from
every sense. Tags are the shared bus. Do not wire rooms in series so each waits
on the last. Do not add a noun for a gap, a season-as-period, or a fifth order.
Formulation is one type; inference and belief are roles on it.

### 7. The person keeps the means.

Offline-first. Optional sync. Plain JSON. The files are never cleared between
days. A life is one long run.

## What “perfect” feels like

Morning. Yesterday was unusual; one optional question, if they want it. A
handoff from last week is already on the Plan rail. The friend offers a mission
or sits quiet. A symbol that has come up three days this week may ask to be
written about. Three declines of the same kind have paused that kind.

Day. They paint, capture, push. A likely total sits beside the plan and does
not replace the estimate. A block ends confirmed, partial, or silent; silence
can be answered in one tap. A task pushed twice is offered a smaller move. A
charged mark is optional. Inbox sorting draws a curve of order made, apart from
points.

Evening. Describe first. Infer second. Conclusions are formulations linked to
what they rest on.

Week. The house proposes new weights and waits. Splits from disagreeing
receptors are listed. A coincidence can be marked. Spectrum names a gate and
may suggest a block they can delete.

Year. Standing rules are kept, revised, or retired. A neglected objective may
come back. They write what they finished on purpose. A season, if they named
one, has its own span and its own ribbon.

A day inside every band, with every block reported, sends no message. Hiding
Meaning leaves every other room working. No screen says a coincidence caused
anything. No tab says the book.

That is the perfected house: honest maps, a loop that steers, and a bench for
meaning the loop cannot own.
