VIEW-SPECIFIC
Scheduler — Funnel. Views: Funnel / Gantt / Dependencies. Periods: Always / Year / Month / Week / Day. Available Tasks + Filters & Sort. With a selection: N selected, Deselect all (clears checks only), Remove from Scheduler. Task rows drag onto two columns of cards: This Year, This Month, Next Month, This Week, Next Week, Today (real local date), Tomorrow (real local date), Eventually / Later (Next Actions list eventually, no period). Dragging one selected task schedules the whole selection; empty selection or a drag outside the selection schedules only the dragged task. Empty cards say Empty. An unfinished past period rolls up one level (day→week→month→year→unscheduled) unless it was pushed; prior placements stay visible in gray past cells.
