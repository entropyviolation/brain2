GLOBAL CHROME (all screens)
  Pinned full-width mill title bar. Tabs: Home · Lists · Docs · Scheduler · Operations · Modules · Analytics.
  Home sub-tabs: Habits · Plan · To Do · Goals · Tracking.

VIEW-SPECIFIC
Tracking log hides the pen desk. The fascia keys are Time Grid · Activity Log · Day Log · Tracking log, with the settings gear at the end of that row. Day summary stays under the log.

Add a line. Mode keys on one row: Event · Switch · Intake · Spent · Note · Thought process. The clock stays visible. Estimated and Unknown are mutually exclusive checkboxes. Add is the raised key at the end of the field row.

Spent: Amount (dollars and cents, a $ in the sunken field), On (what it was spent on), Source (cash, card, account, person, or a name already used). An amount that is empty, zero, or other text leaves Add disabled. A typed non-amount says the field wants dollars and cents, such as 4.50.

The Spent shelf is the first list. A sunken ledger shows the day’s total, then each source, and each thing when more than one thing was bought. A row is the clock, the amount, what, and the source. A known clock opens the block editor, which edits amount, spent on, and source. An unknown clock has Edit and Remove.

Food, Drink, and Drugs stay their own shelves. An empty shelf says what is quiet (Nothing eaten this day, and the same voice for the others). A filled shelf shows its count. Events, Thought process, and Switch follow. Counts sits under the composer. Cycle stays hidden until Enable cycle tracking is on.
