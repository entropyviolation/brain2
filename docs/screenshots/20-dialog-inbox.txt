VIEW-SPECIFIC
Inbox — Clarify Your Ideas. Inbox and Monkey brain partitions. Search ideas is pinned at the top of the list; the list scrolls and the foot stays in view. Required: after a move, the scroll offset stays. The rows that remain stay in view. Do not remount the scroller or zero its scrollTop. That is the preferred behavior for every inbox bulk move, including Transfer to log, and for any later action that removes or updates rows in that list. Each row: check, title, time, age chip. Pencil and trash appear on hover and on the caret row. Foot: Select all, Select N, Select unsorted, Deselect, All/Dated/Bare, Apply list, Due, File, Transfer to log, Monkey brain, Bulk edit, Delete, Merge, Walk or Walk selected. Day plates. Undo banner.

Clarify idea (pencil on a row) is a nested sheet on the same mill, one column: name, assigned lists, list well, notes, Advanced — attributes, Discard idea / Cancel / Save & Clarify. Smart-parse chips stay under the subtitle. Walk uses that same sheet. Under the walk subtitle, a quiet line shows the stored door when the idea has one: BIM, Quick Add, Bulk Add, From notes, Phone Notes, or Scheduled, plus the detail that door kept. An idea with no stored door shows nothing. The pencil sheet does not show that line. Assigned lists sit on a plate named for their folder (`House`, or `Life \ Writing` when that name is shared). Lists with no folder have no plate. Double-click an assigned chip to preview that list on top of the sheet; the × removes the list. The folder name is not a button. List names in the well stay as written.
