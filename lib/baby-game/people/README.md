# `lib/baby-game/people/` — humans for the Baby animal game

_Last mapped: 2026-11-03_

The Center of The World is the game's one human: **a pixie**. Pale skin, brown eyes, wavy strawberry blonde hair, small pointed ears, a black corset, a layered black skirt, a scarf, a silver chain belt with O rings, white go-go boots. She is drawn in the manner of Stardew Valley.

## Two ways she is made

1. **A snapped ComfyUI render (what plays today).** `gen-people.ts` holds her front, side and back as 32×32 letter grids with a palette of her own. It is generated by `scripts/baby-snap-person.mjs` from `gen-people.json` and the liked renders in `refs/` (`center-a.png` front, `center-b.png` side and back). Her colors are k-means over the figures, with letters from U+0200 up, added to `SPRITE_PALETTE` by `registerColors`. She has no hand-drawn poses, so `walk.ts` and `idle.ts` animate her from the stills. A generation wins over the drawer, like a liked generation wins over a hand-drawn animal.
2. **The drawer (fallback, and the way to endless people).** `spec.ts` is a closed `PersonSpec` (build, skin, hair style and color, eyes, mouth, top, bottom, shoes, hat, extras). `draw.ts` paints it in layers by material with four-tone ramps from `color.ts`, in three views and these poses: `stand`, `walk0..3`, `blink`, `breathe`, `reach`, `wave`, `cheer`. `parsePerson` clamps anything unknown. It is rougher than the snapped render. Set `localStorage["brain2-baby-game-player-drawn"] = "1"` to see it in play.

## Files

```
spec.ts          PersonSpec, the closed lists, CENTER_SPEC (the pixie), parsePerson
color.ts         named colors, skin tones, hue-shifted four-tone ramps
draw.ts          the drawer: spec + view + pose to a 32×32 letter grid
player.ts        her look: the snapped render if present, else the drawer; pose source for walk.ts
registry.ts      palette sink and pose sources (imports nothing, so sprites.ts and walk.ts can reach it)
gen-people.json  which figures in refs/ are which view, height, flip, and the eye color she must have
gen-people.ts    GENERATED by scripts/baby-snap-person.mjs. Do not edit.
refs/            the liked renders
people.test.ts   tests
```

## Making or changing her

Queue renders: `node scripts/baby-gens-queue.mjs person-center` (tall) or `person-center-chibi` (big head, brown eyes weighted, a text pass and an img2img pass from `center-a.png`). Number the figures with `node scripts/baby-snap-person.mjs --parts <png> out.png`, edit `gen-people.json`, then run `node scripts/baby-snap-person.mjs preview.png`.

**Eyes.** The model drifts on eye color (it gave green for brown). The prompt weights brown and the snapper recolors green or teal to the manifest's `eyes`. `people.test.ts` fails if her palette reads green or teal.

## Planned

- A chibi render (bigger head) so her face survives at 28 rows. At that height the tall render loses its eyes. Chibi candidates are queued as `sprite-person-chibi*`.
- Hand-made action poses for the snapped render (reach, wave, cheer), wired from the gather and talk actions in `BabyGame.tsx`. The drawer has them; the snapped render does not.
- A describe-a-person BIMBY: text to `PersonSpec` with a keyword fallback, like `generateEconomy`, plus a lab page to try it. Any person could then be generated, or sent to ComfyUI with a prompt built from the same spec and snapped the same way.
