# Perfume trials

Ten-gram concentrates from the cart, so these can be smelled. Pyramids
below are the published note lists (Fragrantica, or the brand page when
it states the notes). The grams are a trial, not a claim to match the
juice in the bottle.

Extra drop-based and honey-benzoin ways to try the same profiles are in
[perfume-experiments.md](perfume-experiments.md).

Weigh a **10.00 g** concentrate. Then **3 g concentrate + 17 g Base de
Parfum**. Age the spray **2–4 weeks**. Where the materials are skin-safe
at this dose, a roller is **2 g concentrate in 8 g jojoba**.

Jasmine sambac on the cart is already the 10% dilution. Weigh that
bottle, not a neat absolute. Labdanum is the 10% dilution. Birch tar is
the 10% dilution. Norlimbanol is not in these trials. If a later trial
uses it, dilute it to 10% first.

The 1 ml bottles cannot fund every trial. Vanilla CO2, tonka, Turkish
rose, and bitter almond are one milliliter each. Make the trial you
want to wear. Vanillin can stand in for vanilla CO2 in the others.
Patch-test jasmine, ylang, tonka, pink pepper, clove, and birch tar.
Bergamot in every trial is the FCF oil. Grapefruit is not in these
concentrates. The Allure Homme Sport Superleggera lean can take it
at night, or leave those grams on bergamot FCF for daylight. Sweet
orange is not in them either. It stays off sunlit skin.

The standing list is [safety-officer.md](safety-officer.md).

## Chart-led pair

Read from the compare chart, longest bar first. Both longevity and sillage
are moderate. Illicit rates about 4.05. Alien Goddess Intense about 4.13.
Illicit (Jimmy Choo): honey, sweet, citrus, white floral, amber, woody,
fresh spicy, caramel. Ginger and bitter orange; jasmine, orange blossom,
rose; honey, amber, caramel, vanilla, sandalwood, cashmir wood.
Alien Goddess Intense (Mugler): vanilla, white floral, amber, coconut,
floral, sweet, balsamic, woody. Coconut and bergamot; jasmine and jasmine
tea; vanilla, benzoin, cashmir wood. Shared: jasmine, vanilla, cashmeran,
and a sweet amber glow. Illicit is honey, citrus, ginger, rose, and caramel.
Goddess is vanilla-first, with coconut, bergamot, jasmine tea, benzoin, and
less spice. The two tables are the cart-only reading. In those tables, honey
and caramel are benzoin, labdanum 10%, and vanillin or vanilla CO2. There is
no honey absolute in those tables. Bitter orange and orange blossom there are
petitgrain bigarade. Sandalwood there is amyris. Cashmir wood is a small
Cashmeran. Bergamot is FCF only. Fractionated coconut oil is odorless. It is
not a coconut perfume. The extra-bottle variants under the tables use ethyl
maltol, a honey accord, Australian sandalwood, cold-pressed bitter orange
for night and soap, Coconut II, and a green-tea accord. Sambac 10% is the
right jasmine for both. Grandiflorum would be a different, heavier jasmine,
and it is not a purchase. Sambac is in fractionated coconut and clouds
alcohol; the spray can still use a modest dose, and the roller is the better
home for a heavy jasmine dose. The 1 ml vanilla CO2 funds only one of these
two. Vanillin carries the other. Weigh 10.00 g, then 3 g in 17 g Base de Parfum.

## Safety

These trials are leave-on. Clove in the fireplace concentrate is 0.10 g,
under 0.5% of both the spray and the roller. Birch tar enters only as
the 10% dilution, at 0.05 g. Bitter almond is the Eden FFPA bottle, kept
at 0.08 g in the Allure trial and at 0.15–0.20 g in the cherry and
Hypnotic Poison trials, and it is not food. Tonka is not food. Ylang in any
roller here stays at or under about 0.8% of the finished oil. Iso E
Super is not in the chart-led Illicit trial. The Allure Homme Sport
trial uses 0.60 g of it in the alcohol perfume. It stays out of the
shampoo bar, the castile, and the washer. Do not rinse a beaker of
concentrate down the sink. The Illicit extra-bottle variant is the only
trial with cold-pressed bitter orange. That peel is phototoxic. It is
for night and soap, not sunlit skin, and the dose above stays under
1.25%. Bergamot in every trial, including Goddess, stays FCF.
