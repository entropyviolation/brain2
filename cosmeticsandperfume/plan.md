## Preservative

PE 9010 (phenoxyethanol and ethylhexylglycerin), 0.5–1%, never over 1.1%.
The shampoo bar and the glitter mist use 1%. The serum uses 1%. The dish brick
uses 0.5%, because 0.2% is under the floor. Pre-disperse it when the water
phase is at the solubility ceiling. The hair mist, the solid perfume, and the
shimmer spray are anhydrous. They get vitamin E as an antioxidant, not a
preservative. Liquid Germall Plus stays off the cart.

## Order of work

1. Buy the scale, gloves, mask, and distilled water.
2. Hair mist. Anhydrous, small, and it teaches the scale.
3. Solid perfume. Same melt, smaller tin.
4. Shimmer spray. Watch the 90°C melt. Test the mister with HiLite Copper.
5. Glitter mist. Water, preservative, its own nozzle.
6. Serum. pH strip. Wait for Aristoflex to hydrate.
7. Shampoo bar. Mask on for the powders.
8. Dish brick. Separate bowls. No aluminum.
9. Perfume accords, one at a time, written down.
10. Perfume trials from [perfume-trials.md](perfume-trials.md). One concentrate at a time.
11. Laundry and dryer last.

## Shelf

Anhydrous oils and the solid perfume: about a year, cool and dark.
Water mists and the serum: use while they smell and look right, and keep them
out of the sun. The dish brick dries for two days before it lives by the sink.
The shampoo bar dries in the soap dish between showers.
