# Dry argan oil hair mist

Humblebee & Me formula. A 25 g batch, just under 30 ml. Anhydrous, so
microbes have nothing to eat and a broad-spectrum preservative is not
part of this bottle. Vitamin E is here to slow rancidity, not to preserve
against mold.

Use on wet or dry hair. Mist the hair, or mist your hands and pat it on.
Kept cool and dry it should last at least a year. If it smells like old
nuts or crayons, the oils have oxidized. Throw it out.

## 25 g batch

| Grams | Percent | Ingredient |
|---|---|---|
| 17.3 | 69.2% | Cyclomethicone, the Lotioncrafter LC995 bottle |
| 2.5 | 10% | C12-15 alkyl benzoate |
| 5.0 | 20% | Argan oil |
| 0.125 | 0.5% | Vitamin E MT-50 |
| 0.075 | 0.3% | Fragrance or essential oil |

Weigh into the 30 ml mister, cap, shake. No heat. Cyclomethicone
evaporates and its flash point is 77°C (169°F). Heating it is how it
disappears or catches fire.

The 0.075 g of scent is a few drops, weighed. Hinoki, lavender, or a
thread of ylang belongs here. Birch tar, clove, and neat jasmine do not.

## Why each ingredient is there

Cyclomethicone is the light volatile carrier. It spreads the oil, then
evaporates, and leaves a thin film of argan instead of a greasy coat.
“Cyclomethicone” on a label can mean a blend of D4, D5, and D6. Health
Canada has raised concern about D4 accumulating in the environment.
Buy Lotioncrafter LC995. Its spec is at least 96% cyclopentasiloxane
(D5), D4 at or under 0.099%, and up to 4% D6. INCI to print:
Cyclopentasiloxane. The buy is 8 oz, $7.95, about 227 g, which is the
smallest size. It cannot ship by Priority Mail or international mail.
Let that order travel on the carrier Lotioncrafter allows.

C12-15 alkyl benzoate is the light ester that keeps a little slip after
the silicone leaves. Lotioncrafter Ester AB, 8 oz, $11.95, is the
smallest size. One mist uses 2.5 g, so the bottle will outlast the
argan. It is also a lightener in lotions later, inside its 0.5–15%
range. This mist uses it at 10%.

Argan is the oil that stays on the hair. Eden Botanicals organic argan,
30 ml, $13.50 (catalog of 2 October 2026). Five grams per batch. At about
0.91 g/ml, 30 ml is about five mists. Jojoba, broccoli seed oil, or camellia can
replace it and will smell and feel different. Jojoba is already in the
perfume cart. Argan is on the list because this formula is built on it.
Do not also buy camellia and broccoli seed.

Vitamin E MT-50 is the same 1 oz bottle as the rest of the pantry
($12.95). This formula wants 0.125 g per 25 g batch.

## Substitutions that stay light, and ones that do not

Interchangeable with LC995: another cyclopentasiloxane. Dimethicone
1.5 cSt is the only dimethicone light enough. Dimethicone 350 and other
heavy grades will not evaporate and will not make a dry mist.

Plant alternatives named for this job: LuxGlide N5 and LexFeel N5
(diheptyl succinate and capryloyl glycerin/sebacic acid copolymer).
They are the right viscosity on paper and they are not silicones. The
finished mist will be a little heavier, and some batches have a faint
plastic note that fades. Buy one of those only if you are skipping
silicone. Do not buy it on top of LC995.

Isododecane is even more volatile than cyclomethicone. A blend near
90% isododecane and 10% light ester is a possible stand-in. It is still
a petrochemical with its own flash point.

Isoamyl laurate, coco-caprylate, isopropyl myristate, and hemisqualane
can replace the alkyl benzoate. They cannot replace the 69%
cyclomethicone. At that dose they make a hair oil, not a dry mist.

## Silicone, without the myths

Silicones do not suffocate skin or hair. The useful critiques are about
persistence, not about a film that blocks oxygen. Lab Muffin’s
silicone mythbusting is the short version: they are not biodegradable
in the compost sense, and they do break down toward silica, carbon
dioxide, and water. D4 is the one to avoid. D5 is the one in this
bottle. This mist is leave-on. Most of the carrier leaves from the hair
into the air. Do not rinse a leftover bottle down the drain.

Stored cool, dark, and away from sparks, the cyclomethicone itself
keeps indefinitely. The argan is what ages. A year is the planning
number for the finished mist.

## Safety

Do not heat this bottle. LC995 flashes at 77°C. The D4 cap at 0.099% is
an environmental limit. Do not pour leftover cyclomethicone down the sink.
Do not spray the mist into your eyes. A thread of ylang in the 0.075 g
scent line stays under the leave-on ceiling. D5 is not given a cancer
label in this folder.
