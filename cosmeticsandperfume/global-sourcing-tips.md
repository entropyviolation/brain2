# Global sourcing tips

Logged from the 5 October 2026 paste. Every tip is kept, including the buys
this pantry does not make. Three verdicts:

- **ADOPT** — safer or cheaper, and it fits the cart.
- **ALREADY IN THE PLAN** — the material or habit is already sized and sourced.
- **DO NOT DO** — the buy, the claim, or the total stays off the cart.

AliExpress glass is a cheaper look only after a leak test, a metal-pump check,
and a scale check. It does not replace the bottle lines. Aroma chemicals stay
at Perfumer’s Apprentice. Named naturals stay at Eden Botanicals.
