# Cedar and palmarosa solid perfume

An anhydrous tin. No broad-spectrum preservative. Grapeseed, beeswax, and
Virginia cedarwood are already on the cart. Palmarosa and cardamom are the
new bottles.

## One tin

| Grams | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 8 | Grapeseed oil |
| 2 | Beeswax |
| 0.24 | Virginia cedarwood, about 8 drops |
| 0.12 | Palmarosa, about 4 drops |
| 0.12 | Cardamom, about 4 drops |

A drop is about 0.03 g. Drops vary with the orifice and the oil. Weigh them
if the scale can see 0.12 g. The batch is about 10.5 g before it cools.
A quarter-ounce tin holds about 7 g. This batch fills one tin generously, or
it needs a second tiny tin.

Melt the grapeseed and the beeswax over medium heat. Cool slightly. Stir in
the essential oils. Pour. Let it set. Wear it on the wrists and behind the ears.

Vitamin E is optional. The tin is small and it gets used up. The MT-50 bottle
can spare a drop if you want it. It is the same antioxidant as the hair mist,
not a preservative.

## Safety

Anhydrous. No PE 9010 in this tin. Palmarosa is a low-risk sensitizer. Tisserand’s
dermal maximum is 6.5%. This tin is about 1% palmarosa. Cardamom is high in
1,8-cineole. Keep it off the face of infants and young children. Patch-test
if your skin is reactive. The melt is hot wax. Keep it off skin until it has set.

## Ambery vanilla solid

A 10.00 g anhydrous tin. Grapeseed is 6.11 g, reduced from the 8 g cedar
ratio, so the scent fits inside 10.00 g. Beeswax stays at 2 g. Grapeseed
and beeswax are already on the cart. Every material below is already on
the cart.

This tin bridges Jimmy Choo Illicit and Mugler Alien Goddess Intense.
Illicit opens on ginger and bitter orange, then jasmine sambac, orange
blossom, and rose, and dries to honey, amber, caramel, vanilla, sandalwood,
and cashmere wood. Goddess Intense opens on bergamot, then jasmine, and
dries to vanilla, benzoin, and cashmeran. Honey and caramel here are
benzoin, a little labdanum 10%, and vanilla. Petitgrain is the bitter-orange
leaf and the orange blossom. Ginger stays small. Turkish rose is a trace.
Amyris is the sandalwood. Cashmeran stays very small, so the drydown stays
golden. Bergamot FCF carries the Goddess opening.

This tin does not use the extra bottles. Honey and caramel here stay benzoin,
a little labdanum 10%, and vanilla. Petitgrain is the leaf. Amyris is the wood
in the wax. The floral is sambac 10%. Grandiflorum would be a different, heavier
jasmine. Jasmine sambac on the cart is the 10% dilution in fractionated coconut.
That oil has no coconut smell. It is fine in the wax. Do not put Coconut II in
this tin.

## Amber cherry solid

A 10.00 g anhydrous tin in the Lost Cherry and Dossier Ambery Cherry
direction. Grapeseed is 6.16 g, down from 8 g, so the tin stays at 10.00 g.
Beeswax stays at 2 g. What you should smell is cherry fragrance oil, a low
bitter almond, a thread of Turkish rose, and a benzoin-tonka wood.

Lost Cherry lists bitter almond, black cherry, rose, vanilla, tonka,
sandalwood, and benzoin among its notes. Dossier’s Ambery Cherry lists
cherry, almond, rose, vanilla, and tonka. Amyris is the sandalwood.
Labdanum 10% is a thread of amber. The weights follow that chart with the
jasmine, cedar, and hedione of the spray trial left out, and with bitter
almond held far under the 0.15 g cap for this tin.

Peru balsam stays out. Cade stays out. Clove stays out. Birch tar stays out.
The cart has no black-cherry accord, no cherry liqueur, no plum, and no
cinnamon.

## Safety for the ambery vanilla and amber cherry tins

Anhydrous. No PE 9010. These tins are leave-on.

Bergamot is the FCF oil. Patch-test jasmine, tonka, and bitter almond. The
bitter almond is the Eden bottle with the prussic acid removed. It is still
benzaldehyde, and it is not for eating. Tonka is not for eating. Vanillin
can stand in for vanilla CO2. Vanilla CO2, tonka, Turkish rose, and bitter
almond are 1 ml bottles, shared with the trials.

Jasmine sambac 10% is still jasmine. The bridge tin holds 0.04 g of absolute.
The Goddess lean holds 0.05 g. The Illicit lean holds 0.015 g. That figure
is the whole tin. A swipe puts a fraction of it on skin.

Clove is not in these tins. Birch tar is not in these tins. The melt is hot
wax. Keep it off skin until it has set.
