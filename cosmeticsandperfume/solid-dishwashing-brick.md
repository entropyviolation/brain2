# Solid dishwashing brick

Humblebee & Me, 25 May 2020.
https://www.humblebeeandme.com/solid-dishwashing-brick/
A 50 g batch. This is a dish cleaner. It is a cleaning project, not a skin wash.

The published preservative is 0.1 g (0.2%) Liquid Germall Plus. PE 9010 at 0.2%
is below its 0.5% minimum. A 0.1 g swap does not preserve a 50 g brick.
Marie’s own note is that the brick is very likely self-preserving: low water,
high anionic surfactant, high pH. She still adds a preservative because the
brick gets wet in the sink.

This plan raises PE 9010 to 0.5% (0.25 g) and takes the extra 0.15 g from the
distilled water. Water goes from 2.8 g to 2.65 g. The wet ingredients still
total 6.95 g, so the press sees the same amount of liquid.

## 50 g batch

| Grams | Percent | Ingredient |
|---|---|---|
| 28.20 | 56.4% | Sodium coco sulfate, powder |
| 13.50 | 27% | Washing soda (sodium carbonate) |
| 1.35 | 2.7% | Iota carrageenan |
| 2.65 | 5.3% | Distilled water |
| 2.20 | 4.4% | Caprylyl/capryl glucoside |
| 1.85 | 3.7% | Vegetable glycerin |
| 0.25 | 0.5% | PE 9010 |

Mask and nitrile gloves. Weigh the three powders and mix them. Massage in the
wet phase. Press hard. Marie’s cube mold was 55 psi. At home, a firm measuring
cup lined with parchment, pressed with the next size down. A floppy silicone
mold bows and will not pack the brick. Fresh bricks are fragile the way a bath
bomb is fragile. Dry at least 48 hours. Rub a wet sponge on the brick. Store it
in a stainless bowl or the soap dish. A humid kitchen needs the dish.

SCI is the shampoo-bar surfactant. SCS is this brick. They are different powders.
SLS is not the default. Baking soda is a different, weaker chemical. Washing
soda stays sodium carbonate. Caprylyl/capryl glucoside is not the polysorbate 20
already on the cart. Lotioncrafter’s SurfPro CG is coco-glucoside. SurfPro CC-6
is PEG-6 caprylic/capric glycerides. Neither one is this line.

Modernist Pantry’s 50 g iota at $9.99 lists carrageenan, maltodextrin, and
dextrose. That is a cut culinary powder. The buy is the 100 g Chem-Impex jar.

No bottle. Parchment and a firm cup are the mold. The slotted soap dish already
on the grocery list can hold the finished brick.

## Safety

Gloves and a dust mask while the powders are open. SCS and carrageenan dust
do not belong in the lungs. Washing soda is alkaline. Keep the brick off
aluminum. Keep it out of the dishwasher. Do not use it as a face or body wash.
The sink is the place it gets wet, which is why the preservative is at 0.5%
and not at Marie’s 0.2% Germall line.
