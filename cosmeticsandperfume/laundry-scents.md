# Laundry scents

Other smell experiments are in [perfume-experiments.md](perfume-experiments.md).
The wash concentrates stay in this file. What the machine is washed with
is in [laundry-detergent.md](laundry-detergent.md). The 30 g castile line
below is a scent portion, not that detergent.

Cart readings of a clean soapy laundry smell and a sweeter fruity-floral
laundry smell. Weigh each as a **10.00 g** concentrate. Every material
is already on the cart. These are wash scents.

The standing review is [safety-officer.md](safety-officer.md).

## Tide-shaped, 10.00 g

Clean, soapy, aldehydic linen: citrus, a white floral lift, soap, musk.

The classic Tide bite is aliphatic aldehydes. Those aldehydes are not on the cart.

| Material | Grams |
|---|---|
| Distilled lemon | 1.60 |
| Bergamot FCF | 1.40 |
| Lavender Maillette | 2.00 |
| Hedione | 2.60 |
| Ethylene brassylate | 2.20 |
| Virginia cedarwood | 0.20 |
| Total | 10.00 |

Hedione carries the white floral lift. Virginia cedar is a thread.
Vanillin stays out of this concentrate.

### Detergent versus dryer ball

Musk, hedione, and a little cedar survive the wash. Citrus tops are mostly gone
after the rinse, so the detergent can carry more citrus. The dryer-ball dose is
the table below: musk, hedione, and cedar, with no lemon.

Lemon smells harsh when heated. Peppermint stays out. Iso E Super stays out of
the wash water. Clove and birch tar stay out.

Wash: 0.15–0.30 g of Fresher citrus Tide, or of Tide-shaped, per 30 g unscented
portion. That portion goes in the wash.

Dryer balls: 6–8 drops of this concentrate per load.

Dryer ball, 10.00 g

| Material | Grams |
|---|---|
| Hedione | 4.50 |
| Ethylene brassylate | 5.20 |
| Virginia cedarwood | 0.30 |
| Total | 10.00 |

## Gain-shaped, 10.00 g

Sweeter and more perfume-like: fruity floral laundry, often read as apple-floral musk.

There is no apple material on the cart.

| Material | Grams |
|---|---|
| Sweet orange | 2.60 |
| Lavender Maillette | 1.70 |
| Rose geranium | 1.70 |
| Ylang ylang complete | 0.40 |
| Ethylene brassylate | 3.40 |
| Vanillin | 0.20 |
| Total | 10.00 |

Sweet orange is the rinse-off fruit. Ylang complete at 0.40 g is the modest dose.
Vanillin is the pinch. Warm and stir until the grains dissolve. A grain that
remains stays off the dryer ball.

Sweet orange and ylang go in the wash: 0.15–0.30 g of the table above per 30 g
unscented portion. The dryer ball gets brassylate and a little vanillin, 6–8
drops per load. Heat makes vanillin and ylang louder, so ylang stays off the
ball. The grains still dissolve before this touches a dryer ball.

## Use

| Use | Dose |
|---|---|
| Dryer balls | 6–8 drops of a dryer-ball concentrate per load, not 30. Tide ball is hedione, brassylate, and cedar. Gain ball is brassylate and vanillin. No lemon on the ball. |
| Unscented Dr. Bronner’s, 30 g | 0.15–0.30 g concentrate (0.5–1%). That portion goes in the wash. |
| Blotter | A drop of concentrate in a little Base de Parfum, on a strip. |
| Body mist at 5% | A bad idea. These are wash scents. |

Drops mean the finished concentrate. Ylang on clothes is fine at this dilution.
Do not put neat ylang oil on the balls.

## Safety

Grapefruit and expressed citrus stay out of anything that sits on skin in the sun.
Laundry rinse-off can use sweet orange. Bergamot here is FCF. Lemon is distilled.

Iso E Super stays out. Wash-off, aquatic toxicity.

Clove, birch tar, and labdanum stay out.

Peppermint can smell like toothpaste in laundry. Leave it out. Optional: one
labeled test vial, 0.05 g peppermint in place of 0.05 g lavender, still 10.00 g.

Hedione and ethylene brassylate are in these wash scents at the grams above.
They stay out of the shampoo bar. Wipe a concentrate spill into the trash.
