## Suppliers

- Liberty Natural for the 22 naturals and carriers that beat Eden on a small bottle. Eden Botanicals stays the counter for CO2 extracts, absolutes, hinoki wood, distilled lemon, vetiver, pepper, cardamom, and amyris.
- Perfumer’s Apprentice for aroma chemicals and Base de Parfum.
- Lotioncrafter for surfactants, butters, waxes, PE 9010, and the hair-mist silicone.
- Grocery and pharmacy for distilled water, glycerin, castile, rice flour, and washing soda.
- TKB for Crystal Clear Wax and HiLite Copper.
- Mad Micas for the plastic-free sparkle.
