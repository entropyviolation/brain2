# Supply coverage

One batch of each project, then how far the planned jar goes. “Four” means four
batches of every project that uses the ingredient. Perfume use is extra and is
called out when it is the reason for the bottle.

Weights treat 1 oz as 28.35 g and 1 lb as 454 g. Liquids counted in grams use
the milliliters when the density is close to 1, and a density note when it is not.
