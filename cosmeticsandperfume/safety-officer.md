# Safety officer

Standing review for this folder. It checks the formulas and the cart, then
points at the file that should carry the warning. A warning lands in a
project file only when that formula actually uses the material.

Checked against the formulas in this folder and against ordinary cosmetic
limits: Tisserand on dermal maxima and phototoxicity, IFRA on leave-on
categories, Health Canada’s concern about D4, the CIR assessment of
synthetic fluorphlogopite, and the PAH load in crude cade and pine tar.

If a material is only a sensitizer, an irritant, or an environmental
problem, that is the word used here. It is not upgraded to a cancer warning.

The 5 October 2026 pass sets IFRA 51st Amendment limits against the formula
percentages in
[research/safety-evidence-2026-10-05.md](research/safety-evidence-2026-10-05.md).
Where that note and this table disagree on a number, the evidence note is
the later check.

## What this pass checks

- Carcinogens and suspected carcinogens, only where the material itself
  carries that record.
- Cyanide in bitter almond that is not prussic-acid-free.
- Phototoxic citrus.
- Sensitizers and phenols, including leave-on ceilings.
- Caustic cleaners.
- Environmental persistence, and what must not go down the sink.
- Fire and flash point.
- Eyes and lungs: powders, mists, and nozzles.
- Cats and birds.
- The serum’s gel, pH, and allantoin limit.

## Not labeled carcinogenic

Cyclopentasiloxane (the D5 in LC995), CoQ10, niacinamide, refined shea,
grapeseed, and PE 9010 are not given a cancer label here. D5’s live issue
in this cart is the D4 cap and the instruction not to pour it down the sink.
PE 9010 is the preservative that replaced the formaldehyde donor. CoQ10,
niacinamide, shea, and grapeseed are ordinary cosmetic ingredients in these
formulas. Synthetic fluorphlogopite is not treated as crystalline silica.
A supplier Prop 65 line about trace residuals in a raw powder is a
disclosure, not a finding that the finished soap or serum causes cancer.

## Candles and incense

A beeswax candle, or a simple unscented candle, is the clean burn.
Lighting an essential oil does not make it non-toxic. The low-smoke
incense option is heating resin on an electric warmer.
