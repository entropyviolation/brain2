# Glitter body and hair mist

Plastic craft glitter stays out. So does metal-film glitter. The sparkle
is synthetic fluorphlogopite from Mad Micas, the sample bag of Sparkle
Plenty, $1.49. It is listed as plastic-free and metal-free, and as
permitted for external use. A sample is about a teaspoon, which is many
30 g bottles.

Synthetic fluorphlogopite is a lab-made mica. The Cosmetic Ingredient
Review panel assessed it as safe in cosmetics, including the small
amount someone might breathe from a pump spray at ordinary cosmetic
levels. Reported perfume use is tiny. This mist uses 0.5%. Do not use
an aerosol. Do not spray toward your face, your eyes, a bird, or a cat.
Keep the dry powder lidded so you are not breathing a cloud of it.
Once it is in liquid, inhalation is the leftover mist, not a puff of
powder.

Particle size on Mad Micas sparkle grades is about 40–200 microns. That
clogs a perfume atomizer. This mist gets its own bottle with a wider
spray, about $4, separate from the cute 10 ml perfume bottles.

Sparkle Me Gold is the warm interference version, same shop, same sample
price, and it adds titanium dioxide and tin dioxide. One sample of
Sparkle Plenty is the clear snow sparkle and the one to buy first. It
will not dye the hair.

## 30 g batch

| Grams | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 27 | Distilled water |
| 1.5 | USP glycerin |
| 0.3 | Polysorbate 20 |
| 0.15 | Sparkle Plenty |
| 0.3 | PE 9010 |
| up to 0.3 | Hinoki or lavender, if you want scent |

Stir the mica into the polysorbate until every speck is wet, then stir
that into the water and glycerin. Add the preservative and the scent.
Cap and shake. Shake again before every spray. Oils floated on water
are how a mist irritates skin. The polysorbate is what keeps the mica
and any oil from sitting on top.

This is a water product. PE 9010 belongs in it. The hair mist does not
get this preservative, because that bottle has no water. The shampoo
bar does, because the betaine and the rice protein bring water with
them.

Mica on the surface of the shampoo bar looks pretty and makes a
sparkling, slippery shower floor. Leave the shimmer in the spray.
Polysorbate 20 on the cart is the 4.5 oz jar, $5.50.

## Safety

Shake, then spray the body or the hair. Keep the mist out of eyes.
Do not aerosolize the dry mica, and do not point it at a cat or a bird.
Sparkle Plenty is synthetic fluorphlogopite. The CIR panel assessed that
material as safe at ordinary cosmetic spray levels. It is not a
crystalline-silica warning. Wet every speck in the polysorbate before it
meets the water. Sparkle Plenty is too coarse for the hair-mist nozzle
and for the shimmer-oil mister. PE 9010 is the preservative. Germall Plus
stays off the cart.
