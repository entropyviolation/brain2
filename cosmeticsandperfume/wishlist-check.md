## Left off

| Requested, then rejected | Why |
|---|---|
| Cade, crude pine tar | Polycyclic aromatic hydrocarbons. |
| Crude bitter almond | Cyanide risk. Only the Eden FFPA bottle is allowed. |
| Peruvian balsam | Allergen. |
| Raw oakmoss | Allergens. Veramoss is the moss note. |
| Ambrette | Ethylene brassylate does that job. |
| Pineapple isolate, blackcurrant bud | Not the same smell, and the bud is a poor buy. |
| Isoamyl laurate as its own bottle | A possible ester swap, not a third carrier. |
| Camellia, rice bran, sweet almond, mango | Extra oils. Grapeseed is the linoleic bottle. |
| High-oleic sunflower, a second sunflower | Lotioncrafter sunflower is the wrong oil. |
| Cocoa, cupuacu, unrefined shea | Refined shea and kokum are the butters. |
| Olivem 1000, candelilla, carnauba | Not in these formulas. |
| Liquid Germall Plus | Formaldehyde donor. PE 9010 is the preservative. |
| Neat jasmine, rose otto, 15 ml santalum album | Amyris is the blender. Australian spicatum is 1 ml at $9.50, a trace, not a 15 ml. |
| Nut or macadamia accord | Left off. |
| Craft glitter, marketplace oils | Plastic glitter and unvetted oils. |
| Ylang II as a second bottle | Ylang complete is the working bottle. |
| Coconut fragrance, gamma-nonalactone | Fractionated coconut oil is odorless. Coconut II fragrance oil, 15 ml, $6.00, is the coconut smell. Gamma-nonalactone was not added. |
| SLS, baking soda as a silent swap | SCS and washing soda are the dish-brick chemicals. |
