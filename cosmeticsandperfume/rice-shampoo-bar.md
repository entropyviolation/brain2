# Rice starch shampoo bar

Humblebee & Me, sulfate-free, no heat. One 100 g batch is one or two
bars. It is also a body bar. The original uses camellia seed oil and
Liquid Germall Plus. This plan uses grapeseed and PE 9010 so the pantry
stays one oil and one preservative.

The bar contains water inside the betaine and the hydrolyzed rice
protein, about 11.5% when it is fresh. It loses about 3% of its weight
in 3–4 days and is hard enough after one day. Wait until day 3 or 4 if
you can. Humid rooms need longer. Dry it on a slotted dish. Between
showers, let it drain. The original formula lands near pH 5.8.

## 100 g batch

| Grams | Ingredient |
|---|---|
| 40 | Sodium cocoyl isethionate, fine powder |
| 23 | Sodium lauryl sulfoacetate |
| 15 | Rice starch, or silky rice flour |
| 11 | Cocamidopropyl betaine, liquid |
| 4 | Hydrolyzed rice protein, liquid |
| 4 | Grapeseed oil |
| 1 | Scent blend below |
| 0.1 | Vitamin E MT-50 |
| 1 | PE 9010 |

Mask on for the powders. Gloves on. Stir the powders. Make a well, add
the liquids, and knead to a stiff dough. Too sticky: more rice flour,
usually because the SCI was coarse. Too dry: a few drops of distilled
water, usually because the SCI was finer than the one the formula was
built on. SCI in this batch is 40%, under the 49.87% rinse-off ceiling,
which is why SLSa is there to carry the rest of the lather. SCI is the
velvet. SLSa is the fluff. Betaine boosts the flash foam and softens
the bar.

Skip the dye. That 0.01 g goes back into the betaine.

Scent, the 1 g: lavender 0.50 g, Virginia cedarwood 0.25 g, ylang
complete or geranium 0.15 g, sweet orange 0.10 g. One gram is enough
to smell after a rinse. The 15-drop perfume copies are for alcohol
perfume, not for this dough.

Glutinous rice flour, Mochiko, and sweet rice flour are the wrong grind.
Rice starch is the better filler if a baking supplier has a small bag.
Flour is the whole grain. Starch is the separated carbohydrate. Either
works. Potato starch has stood in for readers when the rice flour was
gritty.

PE 9010 is phenoxyethanol and ethylhexylglycerin. Use 0.5–1%. The
ceiling is 1.1%. This batch uses 1 g. It is only moderately soluble in
plain water, which is fine in this dough because the surfactants are
right there. Liquid Germall Plus at 0.5 g was the published formula. It
is a formaldehyde donor. One preservative is enough, and PE 9010 is the
one on the cart.

Vitamin E does not stop mold. It keeps the grapeseed from going stale.

## What does not go in the bar

Iso E Super, Hedione, jasmine absolute, rose absolute, labdanum, tonka,
birch tar, cade, fossilized amber. Heat and rinse destroy the expensive
florals, and Iso E Super does not belong in wash-off water. Cypress,
pine, fir, patchouli, lavender, cedar, and a little ylang or geranium
are the oils this bar can actually use.

## Safety

Mask and gloves while the SCI and SLSa are open. Lotioncrafter’s SCI
page discloses trace 1,4-dioxane and ethylene oxide under 1 ppm. That is
a raw-powder line. The finished bar is not being called a carcinogen.
The dust is the practical risk. PE 9010 replaces Liquid Germall Plus.
Germall is a formaldehyde donor and stays off the cart. Sweet orange in
the 1 g scent is a rinse-off dose. Grapefruit and expressed citrus stay
off skin that will see the sun.
