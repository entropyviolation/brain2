# Perfume experiments

Extra ways to try the same profiles. The formulas in
[perfume-trials.md](perfume-trials.md) stay as they are. Nothing here is a
verified match to a brand bottle. These are smell experiments, not treatments.

**A** is the cart. Weigh a **10.00 g** concentrate from bottles already on
the shopping list. Then **3 g concentrate + 17 g Base de Parfum**. Age the
spray **2–4 weeks**. Where the dose is skin-safe, a roller is **2 g
concentrate in 8 g jojoba**.

**B** is the pasted drop recipe at about **0.03 g per drop**. Drops vary
with the orifice and the oil. B is a record. Where a line overdoses, the
sentence under the table is the dose to wear, and that dose is A.

**C** names a material that stays off the cart.

Tare the vial. Weigh grams. Warm thick resins in a hot-water bath. Jasmine
sambac on the cart is already 10% in fractionated coconut. That oil can
cloud a high-alcohol perfume. It is fine in a roller, and it is a known
limit in the spray.

Bergamot is FCF. Lemon is distilled. Grapefruit and sweet orange are not
for sunlit skin. Pink pepper can cross-react with cashew, mango, and
pistachio allergy.

## Safety

Finished leave-on clove stays at or under 0.5%. In a 15% eau de parfum that is about 0.33 g of clove in the 10 g concentrate. The fireplace experiment uses 0.08 g. Birch tar enters only as 0.05 g of the 10% dilution. Cade stays unused. Bitter almond is the FFPA bottle, at 0.15 g in Cinéma and 0.20 g in Hypnotic Poison, both under the 0.30 g start, and it is not food. Tonka is not food. Ylang, when a formula uses it, stays near 0.8% of the leave-on product. Do not rinse a beaker of concentrate down the sink.

## Chanel Allure Homme Sport, botanical roller

The alcohol trial stays in [perfume-trials.md](perfume-trials.md). These rollers sit beside it. They do not replace it. Nothing here is a verified match to the bottle.

A later Eden mandarin would be the sparkling top, and it is not on this order.

The paste called a 10 ml roller about 15%. Thirty-four drops at about 0.03 g a drop is 1.02 g of oil. In a 10 ml roller that is roughly 10% of the fill, not 15%. Each roller below is 9.00 g, jojoba plus scent.

A natural roller sits close to the skin. It needs reapplying. It will not last eight hours. It does not fill a room.

Natural oils are not non-toxic. Patch-test tonka. Bergamot in these rollers is FCF. Expressed bergamot does not go in. Cold-pressed mandarin stays out.

Wipe the tools with alcohol. Blend the base, then the heart, then the top. Base is Java vetiver and tonka or vanilla CO2, then Virginia cedar. Heart is balsam fir or cypress, and black pepper. Top is bergamot FCF. Fill with jojoba. Age it one to two weeks in the dark.

Fractionated coconut oil can replace the jojoba. It is an odorless carrier, not a coconut perfume. Do not buy more of it.
