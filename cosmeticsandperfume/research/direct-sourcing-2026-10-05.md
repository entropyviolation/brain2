# Direct sourcing, 5 October 2026

Cheapest safe path for every material on the deduped cart in [shopping-list.md](../shopping-list.md). This pass goes past Eden Botanicals, Perfumer’s Apprentice, and Lotioncrafter to the wholesaler or mill that will actually sell a small bottle. It does not change a formula or a purchase.

Figures are package prices read from public product pages on 5 October 2026, unless a line says the price is from the same-day note [cheaper-options.md](../cheaper-options.md) and was not re-opened. No price below is guessed. Login walls and “returning soon” pages with no size are quote-only or unavailable.

## 1. Method and assumptions

The buyer is one person in the United States, on Pacific time, making four small batches of each project plus a perfume kit. A useful buy is the jar that covers that use. A cheaper price per kilo that starts at 500 g, 5 lb, or a drum is recorded as a break-even, not as the buy.

- One fluid ounce on Liberty Natural’s tables is 29.57 ml. Half an ounce is 14.79 ml. Comparisons below use those milliliters.
- Organic is kept when the organic ounce is the cheap one. It is dropped when the species, the plant part, and the extraction match and the organic premium is most of the bottle. Skin safety does not require the organic seal on these oils.
- Bergamot on skin in daylight stays bergaptene-free. Lemon that stays on skin stays distilled. Hinoki stays a wood oil of *Chamaecyparis obtusa* until a page says otherwise. Bitter almond stays a bottle that says the prussic acid is removed.
- Shipping is extra. Liberty Natural (Oregon City) charges the carrier rate. It is not folded into the bottle price. Their domestic wholesale minimum is $50, with a $15 surcharge under that. The Liberty basket in section 5 is about $131, so the surcharge does not apply. Nature in Bottle prices are export kilos. They are not a small-batch win after freight.
- “Prior note” is the shopping-list price. Where [cheaper-options.md](../cheaper-options.md) already found a higher live Eden price, the savings use that live price, and the list price is named beside it.

## 2. How to read supplier roles

The hobby bottle is usually three or four steps from the still or the reactor.

| Role | Who | What they will sell a person |
|---|---|---|
| Distiller or molecule plant | Liberty’s own Oregon lavender still; IFF, dsm-firmenich, Takasago, Givaudan, Symrise; Clariant (Aristoflex); schülke (Euxyl PE 9010); silicone plants behind cyclomethicone; Colonial, Stepan, Innospec, Galaxy (surfactants); CP Kelco (carrageenan); glycerin plants | A drum, a tote, or nothing. No public 15 ml price was found. Quote only. |
| Primary importer / wholesaler who repacks ounces | Liberty Natural, Oregon City. Grower of its own lavender, and importer of the other oils. | Ounce prices on an open page. This is the cut that is real for this pantry. |
| Export wholesaler | Nature in Bottle (India). Not the mill. | Usually 250 g to 1 kg and up. Unit price looks low. The cash and the freight do not. |
| Distributor who wants an account | Vigon, Berje, Ventós, Ultra International, Penta, Elan | No public small-pack cart found. Quote only. |
| Specialty importer with named lots | Eden Botanicals | The right counter for CO2 extracts, absolutes, and odd woods. Not the distiller of those bottles. |
| Repacker of manufacturer aroma chemicals | Perfumer’s Apprentice (US). Fraterworks (New Zealand reseller; says it is not an IFF agent). Creating Perfume (US reseller). | The only public 4–15 ml prices with an IFRA sheet. |
| Cosmetic-ingredient shop | Lotioncrafter, Wholesale Supplies Plus, Formulator Sample Shop, Bulk Apothecary / Nature’s Oil, Derma Factors | Small jars of someone else’s chemical. Bulk Apothecary fills glycerin in the US. It is not the glycerin plant. |
| Pigment shop | TKB Trading, Mad Micas | Effect pigments. The pigment makers (Kobo and the like) do not run a 6 g cart. |
| Lab catalog | Chem-Impex | Pure iota carrageenan by the 100 g, not a food blend. |
| Grocery brand | Arm & Hammer washing soda, Dr. Bronner’s, a pharmacy glycerin | The small size. The carbonate and soap plants do not sell a 55 oz box to a person. |

Liberty’s wholesale policy says a certificate of analysis and an SDS are printable on most product pages. The price tables opened today showed the Latin name, origin, and extraction method. They did not show the chromatogram itself. Ask Regulatory before treating a lot as documented. Eden and Perfumer’s Apprentice remain the houses that put IFRA sheets on the aroma-chemical and absolute bottles already in the notes.
