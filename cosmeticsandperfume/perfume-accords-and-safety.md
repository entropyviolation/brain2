# Perfume accords and safety

Trials of the favorite bottles are in [perfume-trials.md](perfume-trials.md).
Extra ways to try them are in [perfume-experiments.md](perfume-experiments.md).
The standing review is [safety-officer.md](safety-officer.md).

Weigh a 10.00 g concentrate. Then either 2 g of it in 8 g jojoba
(perfume oil) or 3 g of it in 17 g Base de Parfum (spray). Let the
spray sit in the dark for two to four days before you judge it, and
two to four weeks before you call it finished. Write grams in the
notebook: name, date, dilution, then how it smelled wet, at one hour,
the next morning, and at one week. Note the skin test.

Alcohol-diluted absolutes go into the perfume sprays. Oil-diluted
absolutes do not. The jasmine sambac 10% is in fractionated coconut.
That oil clouds and separates in alcohol. A spray can still take a
modest dose. A heavy jasmine dose belongs in the jojoba roller.

Leave-on guide: body oil 1–2.5% in jojoba. Perfume oil about 20% in
jojoba. Eau de parfum about 15% in Base de Parfum. Kinder body mist
about 5%. Clove bud stays at or under 0.5% of anything that remains
on skin. Ylang stays near 0.8% on skin. The shampoo bar and the dryer
are rinse-off and can use a simpler blend.

## Clean woods, 10 g

| Material | Grams |
|---|---|
| Virginia cedarwood | 2.00 |
| Iso E Super | 2.50 |
| Hedione | 1.50 |
| Hinoki wood | 1.00 |
| Balsam fir | 0.80 |
| Black spruce | 0.60 |
| Cypress | 0.50 |
| Amyris | 0.50 |
| Ethylene brassylate | 0.40 |
| Norlimbanol, already diluted to 10% | 0.15 |
| Cashmeran | 0.05 |

Virginia cedar is the dry pencil. Atlas cedar is a different oil, sweet
and balsamic, and it is not this line. Norlimbanol, Ysamber, Karmawood,
and Timberol are one family. One 4 ml bottle of Norlimbanol, diluted to
10% the day it arrives, is years. Neat, it blinds the nose. Their own
ceiling is about 2% of a concentrate.

## Burnt, as its own 10 g

Start from 8.90 g of the clean-woods blend. Add guaiacwood 0.40 g,
Java vetiver 0.50 g, black pepper 0.10 g, clove bud 0.05 g, and birch
tar already at 10% 0.05 g. Smell it on a strip before it touches skin.
Cade is not in this accord. Crude cade carries polycyclic aromatic
hydrocarbons. Twelve drops of neat birch tar is a heavy phenolic dose.
The 10% dilution, weighed, is the bottle.

## Men’s citrus wood, 10 g

Bergamot FCF 1.50, grapefruit 1.00 (night, or more bergamot FCF instead),
Hedione 2.00, Iso E Super 2.00, Virginia cedar 1.20, distilled lemon
0.60, ethylene brassylate 0.50, Java vetiver 0.30, pink pepper 0.15,
peppermint 0.10, nutmeg 0.08, Cashmeran 0.08, Veramoss 0.05. Leave
birch tar out. Pink pepper can cross-react with cashew, mango, and
pistachio allergy.

## Laundry and the closet

Tide-shaped and Gain-shaped 10 g concentrates, the dryer-ball dose, and
the castile portion are in [laundry-scents.md](laundry-scents.md). Six
to eight drops of those concentrates per load.

Closet sachet: a spoon of epsom salt, one drop of jasmine sambac 10%,
one drop of cedar, one drop of labdanum 10%. That is the Alien shape
without washing the absolute down the drain.

Castile wash for a woods scent: unscented Dr. Bronner’s, diluted about
1:3 in a 2 oz foamer, about 15 drops total of fir, cedar, and sweet
orange. The laundry-scent castile dose is the one in laundry-scents.md.

## What each precious bottle actually is

- Honeysuckle fragrance oil, 15 ml, $6. There is no ordinary honeysuckle
  essential oil. This accord is the bottle.
- Jasmine sambac 10% in fractionated coconut, about $12.50. Neat sambac
  is about $22 per ml and handles like tar.
- Turkish rose by-absolute, 1 ml, $12. Rose otto is about $70 per ml.
  Rose hydrosol, 4 oz, $15.50, is the rose for the face. Geranium is the
  rose note for soap and laundry.
- Ylang ylang complete, 15 ml, $12.50. Full distillation.
- Labdanum 10%, 30 ml, $10. This is the sweet amber of perfume. It is
  the solvent-extracted resin, already at 10%. Steam-distilled cistus
  leaf oil is lighter and pinene-led. We do not buy that second oil.
- Benzoin absolute, Sumatra, 15 ml, about $11.50. One benzoin. Warm the
  bottle in hot water. Raw resin tears are for charcoal, and they will
  not dissolve in a roller.
- Fossilized amber 5%, 15 ml, $13.50. Smoky tar. A different amber.
- Vanilla CO2, 1 ml, about $9.75, plus a small vanillin for the bar and
  the dryer. A vanilla absolute at about $25 per ml is the repeat.
- Tonka, 1 ml, about $17.75. Fine in a worn perfume. Not a flavoring.
- Bitter almond only if the Eden page says the prussic acid is removed,
  1 ml, $6. Still not for eating. Cherry fragrance oil, 15 ml, $6, is
  the candy cherry. Crude bitter almond can contain cyanide.
- Amyris, 15 ml, $10, is the sandalwood blender. The Illicit extra variant
  uses 1 ml of Australian *Santalum spicatum* at $9.50, as a trace.
  Plantation album is $13.00 for 1 ml, and a 15 ml of album still waits.
- Veramoss, 8 g, $6.75, is the oakmoss note without oakmoss allergens.
- Hinoki wood, 15 ml, $21.75. Plantation sawmill wood, not old growth.
  A calm temple-wood for the diffuser, the bath, and this hair mist.
  Dilute it. It is a pleasure, not a medicine.
- Petitgrain bigarade is the bitter-orange note that can be worn in
  daylight. The night Illicit variant adds 1 ml of cold-pressed bitter
  orange, phototoxic, for night and soap. Bergamot that is not FCF,
  expressed lemon, and grapefruit are phototoxic too.
- Frankincense CO2 India is *Boswellia serrata*, the soap and perfume
  resin, 5 ml, $11. A sweeter carterii is a later bottle for face oil.
- Ethylene brassylate is the musk. Ambrette seed stays unbought.
- Iso E Super stays in alcohol perfume and in candles at a normal
  fragrance load. It stays out of the shampoo bar, the castile, and the
  washer. Hedione and ethylene brassylate stay out of the shampoo bar.
  The laundry concentrates use them only at the doses in
  [laundry-scents.md](laundry-scents.md). Wipe spills into the trash.
  Finished perfume on skin is a trace. A beaker rinsed down the sink
  is the problem.

## Safety

Patch-test clove, jasmine, ylang, tonka, pink pepper, and birch tar on
the inner forearm for 24 hours. If cats or birds share the room, diffuse
lightly and keep clove and birch tar off surfaces they lick. The citrus
warnings, the clove and ylang ceilings, the 10% birch tar, the FFPA
bitter almond, and Veramoss in place of oakmoss are the hazards for this
file. Cade stays unbought. Iso E Super stays out of the wash.
