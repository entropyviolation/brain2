# Shimmery body oil spray

Humblebee & Me, 10 February 2025.
https://www.humblebeeandme.com/shimmery-body-oil-spray/
A 50 g batch, sized for a 60 ml spray bottle. Anhydrous. No broad-spectrum
preservative. It lasts about a year if it stays dry.

The published oil is regular linoleic sunflower at 77.3%. Lotioncrafter’s
sunflower is high-oleic, the wrong oil, and the cart already has grapeseed.
Grapeseed stands in for that 77.3%. One linoleic bottle, not two.

## 50 g spray

| Grams | Percent | Ingredient |
|---|---|---|
| 38.65 | 77.3% | Grapeseed oil |
| 10.00 | 20% | Jojoba, golden |
| 0.25 | 0.5% | Crystal Clear Wax |
| 0.50 | 1% | Fine mica, HiLite Copper |
| 0.35 | 0.7% | Fragrance or essential oil |
| 0.25 | 0.5% | Vitamin E MT-50 |

Crystal Clear Wax is caprylic/capric triglyceride and polyurethane-79. The wax
is about 30% polyurethane-79 and 70% caprylic/capric triglyceride. The 0.15%
of the spray that is not a natural oil is that polyurethane. If the mica
colorants are natural and the scent is an essential oil, the rest of the
formula is the 99.85% natural figure Marie gives. A fragrance oil changes that.

The heated phase has to reach about 90°C (194°F). A water bath does not get
there. Stovetop, low, or a heat-safe cup on a tray in an oven around 100°C.
Watch it. Crystal Clear Wax can sit in the pan as an unmelted blob. Stir until
it is gone. Take the pan off the heat, stir in the mica, and keep stirring
down to room temperature. Then the fragrance and the vitamin E.

Delicate oils go in with the mica, not through the 90°C heat. Jojoba is in
the heated phase in the published formula because it is the 20% carrier.
Argan stays out of this spray.

Fragrance at 0.7% needs an IFRA category 5A limit at or above 0.7%. Use
lavender, hinoki, bergamot FCF, or Virginia cedar. Stock Fragrance Luxe stays
off the cart.

Marie’s per-batch wax cost was about $0.13. At $9.25 for 10 g the sample is
$0.23 per 50 g spray. Use the current sample price.

HiLite Copper is Marie’s example of a mica whose upper particle size sits
under about 60 microns. She lists this color at 10–60 microns, and she says
TKB, Bramble Berry, and YellowBee sell ethically sourced mica. The TKB product
record on 5 October 2026 did not reprint the micron range. It is tagged vegan,
so carmine is not in the listing. Fineness matters more than mined versus
synthetic. Mad Micas Sparkle Plenty is 40–200 microns. It is the water-mist
mica. It clogs a fine mister.

Crystal Clear Wax misbehaves with a high load of caprylic/capric triglyceride,
with C12-15 alkyl benzoate (it blobs), and with mineral oil (the spray goes
too thin). Do not build this spray on the hair-mist ester or on fractionated
coconut oil.

## 2013 fallback, in the same bottle family

Humblebee & Me, 29 December 2013. No Crystal Clear Wax. The shimmer settles,
and the mister can clog. This is the backup if the wax sample is disappointing.
It is not a second shopping project.

| a pinch | The same HiLite Copper, about 1/16 teaspoon |

The published butter is cupuacu. Marie allows unrefined shea or cocoa in its
place, and she rules out coconut oil. The cart has refined shea, 1 lb, and
kokum. Use 4 g of the refined shea. Expect less scent and a slightly different
thickness. Cupuacu, cocoa, and unrefined shea stay off the cart. A comment on
the post mentions glyceryl stearate from 3% up. Leave that off the cart too.
The planned suspender is Crystal Clear Wax.

Bergamot on the cart is FCF. Lavender is already the 30 ml Maillette bottle.

## Safety

The 90°C heat is this formula, in a watched pan. Do not put cyclomethicone
on that heat. LC995’s flash point is 77°C, and it lives in the hair mist.
Fine mica can still clog the mister. Do not put Sparkle Plenty in this bottle.
Do not aerosolize dry mica. Wet it into the oil. Do not spray the oil into
your eyes. Grapeseed and shea are the carriers. They are not a cancer warning.
Bergamot FCF is the phototoxicity-reduced oil. Expressed bergamot stays off
the cart.
